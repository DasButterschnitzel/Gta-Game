# HAFENGOLD — Story Bible

> *"In Neuhafen gibt es zwei Sorten Gold. Das eine glänzt im Rathaus. Das andere kommt in Bananenkisten."*
> ("Neuhafen has two kinds of gold. One shines in City Hall. The other comes in banana crates.")

Tone: a grounded crime drama with real stakes and loss, wrapped in GTA-style satire of modern German
life — bureaucracy, construction sites that never finish, law-and-order politicians, start-up bros,
crypto, real-estate sharks, and the eternal Döner. All characters, gangs, companies and places are
fictional. Expected rating: 18+ (violence, drug trade, crime, strong language). No sexual content.

---

## The city: Neuhafen
A port city of 1.1 million at the mouth of the river **Lauter**, northern Germany. Rich on paper, rotten
underneath. Three zones, unlocked by the story:

| Zone | Districts | Act |
|---|---|---|
| **Südstadt** (between the Lauter and the Hafenkanal) | **Betonhausen** (GDR-era Plattenbau estate), **Sankt Paula** (the Kiez: bars, clubs, neon), **Bahnhofsviertel** (Hauptbahnhof, pawnshops, kebab shops, the open drug scene) | 1 |
| **Hafen & Industrie** (south of the Hafenkanal) | **Freihafen** (container terminal, red-brick warehouses, gantry cranes, basins), **Rostwerk** (dying steel mill, scrapyards, tank farms) | 2 |
| **Nordstadt** (north of the Lauter) | **Altstadt** (Fachwerk, cobbles, Dom, Rathaus), **Turmviertel** (bank towers), **Sonnenhang** (villas), **Universität**, **Stadtpark**, **Flughafen NHI** (the airport that never opened) | 3 |

At the start the Hafenkanal's bascule bridges are raised ("Brücken hoch — Schiffsverkehr") and the
Lauter bridges are closed for renovation ("Brückensanierung bis voraussichtlich 2031").

## Main characters

**Kai "Falke" Falkner** (31) — *protagonist.* Grew up in Betonhausen with his mother (a night-shift
nurse) and little sister Lena. The best getaway driver Neuhafen ever had — calm, dry humour, says little,
notices everything ("Falke" = falcon). Six years ago he drove for a crew that hit an armoured cash
transporter on the A 26. The police were waiting. He crashed, got caught, kept his mouth shut and did six
years in JVA Neuhafen. His share of the 2.4 million € vanished. His mother died while he was inside.
*Wants:* his share and the name of whoever sold them out. *Needs:* to stop running and protect what's left
of his family.

**Lena Falkner** (26) — Kai's sister. Investigative journalist at the small online outlet *Lauterblick*.
Stubborn, funny, furious at Kai for leaving her alone with their dying mother. She's chasing a story about
the luxury megaproject **Hafenkrone** and the money behind it — which puts her in the crosshairs of the
most powerful man in the city.

**Dennis "Dende" Wagner** (30) — Kai's childhood friend from Block 7. Small-time dealer, big heart, terrible
ideas. Lost his licence ("Lappen weg") so Kai drives his beloved tuned Volksflitzer Golfo GTI, *Gisela*. Comic
relief and Kai's anchor to who he used to be.

**Herbert "Onkel Herbie" Schulz** (63) — the old crew's planner, a father figure to Kai. Runs *Herbies
Leihhaus*, a pawnshop in the Bahnhofsviertel. Cardigan, flat cap, endless stories. Says the police seized
the money. *Secret:* he sold the heist out in exchange for immunity and 400,000 € — and the rest became
someone's seed capital.

**KHK Jens Ruhland** (48) — Kriminalhauptkommissar, organised-crime unit of the LKA. Leather jacket,
moustache, chews nicotine gum, quotes the Basic Law while breaking it. Runs informants like a business.
Achterberg's enforcer inside the police.

**Dr. Konrad Achterberg** (58) — real-estate tycoon, Innensenator (interior senator) and mayoral candidate:
*"Neuhafen. Sicher. Sauber."* Builds the **Hafenkrone** towers and sits on the board of the
never-finished airport NHI. Behind the curtain he is the top of the "Hafengold" pipeline: cocaine through
the port, laundered through construction contracts. The 2.4 million from Kai's heist was his first
investment.

## The gangs (rival pairs)

**Die Lotsen** — *harbour smugglers* (Freihafen). Old-school, family-run, "a handshake is a contract".
Boss **Kapitän Hinrich Tamsen** (67): weathered North Sea captain, pipe, peaked cap, greets everyone with
"Moin" no matter the hour. Business: cigarettes, guns, and — reluctantly — Achterberg's cocaine.
Colours: navy & white. Rival: Eisenwölfe.

**Eisenwölfe MC** — *outlaw bikers* (Rostwerk). Meth ("Crystal") from across the border, stolen premium
cars shipped east in containers. President **Bernd "Bulle" Brückner** (45): huge, bald, braided beard, hates
his nickname because it also means "cop". Colours: black & rust orange. Rival: Die Lotsen.

**Kiez-Kompanie** — *red-light district bosses* (Sankt Paula). Bars, clubs, door security, protection
money. Boss **Ursula "Uschi" Brandt** (52): former European boxing champion "Die Faust von Sankt Paula",
gold tracksuit, owns *Club Knockout*. Tough, fair, sentimental about the old Kiez. Colours: gold & red.
Rival: NEON.

**NEON** — *techno-club pill syndicate* (Betonhausen club scene). Runs the club *Turbine* in an old power
station; ecstasy and ketamine, laundered through the crypto app *KryptoKraft*. Founder **Lennart "Lenny" von
Hoyningen** (29): aristocratic drop-out, round glasses, turtleneck, talks like a pitch deck ("We're
disrupting the nightlife vertical"). Colours: neon green & black. Rival: Kiez-Kompanie.

**Kronwache** — Achterberg's private security company (black SUVs, earpieces). Appears from Act 1's finale.

## Supporting cast
* **Gül Yıldız** ("Mutti Gül") — owns *Gül's Grill* in Betonhausen; Kai rents the room above it
  (first safehouse). Feeds him, scolds him, knew his mother.
* **Kalle** — arms dealer operating out of a Schrebergarten shed (*Kalle's Kleinkaliber*).
* **Ilse Grabowski** (71) — retired telecoms engineer and gifted safe-cracker; Act 3 heist specialist.
* **Dr. Petra Lindqvist** — Achterberg's chief accountant, would-be whistleblower.

## Story arc

### Prologue — "A 26" (six years earlier, playable)
Night, rain, Autobahn. Kai drives the getaway car after the armoured-transporter job. Police are
waiting on every ramp. A desperate chase (driving tutorial) ends in a crash under a bridge. Freeze frame.
**"6 Jahre später."**

### ACT 1 — "Entlassung" (Release) · Südstadt
1. **Freigang** — Kai walks out of JVA Neuhafen. Only Dende is waiting, with Gisela. Kai drives them to
   Betonhausen (driving basics). Dende tells him Lena hasn't spoken a word about him in six years. Gül
   gives Kai the room above the grill. *Safehouse unlocked.*
2. **Altes Revier** — Dende's deliveries around Betonhausen (on-foot, entering cars, markers). NEON pill
   pushers jump Dende at the playground — first fight.
3. **Onkel Herbie** — Kai visits Herbie's pawnshop. Herbie hugs him, swears the police took everything,
   gives him an old pistol "for old times' sake" and a little job to get back on his feet.
4. **Abschleppdienst** — Steal Herbie's client's car from the police impound lot (wanted level, escape,
   first Lackiererei respray).
5. **Die Faust von Sankt Paula** — Herbie introduces Kai to Uschi. Collect protection money from three Kiez
   bars; one owner runs, one sends his bouncers, one pays in Pfandflaschen.
6. **Ticken** — Dende teaches dealing: buy Gras in the Stadtpark-Ost, sell on the Kiez, read the phone
   tips (dealing tutorial; the trading economy opens).
7. **Turbine** — Lenny hires Kai to rush a pill shipment to the club before doors open, with Kiez muscle
   chasing (timed delivery).
8. **Rotlicht, Blaulicht** — A routine traffic stop turns into an arrest: KHK Ruhland. He "finds" a gun in
   Kai's car and makes an offer: work for him, or back to JVA. First job: steal an evidence van from the
   police station yard before it reaches the prosecutor.
9. **Kiezkrieg** — Ruhland makes Kai plant NEON pills in Club Knockout and tip off the Kiez. War breaks out
   between Kiez-Kompanie and NEON (gang members now fight in the streets). Kai has to pick who to help
   when NEON firebombs the club (respect choice).
10. **Schwesterherz** — Lena calls for the first time in six years: she needs a driver to meet a
    whistleblower in the harbour about *Hafenkrone*. It's an ambush by Kronwache. Kai gets her out in a
    chase over the just-lowered canal bridges. Lena says the name: **Achterberg**. *Hafen & Industrie
    unlocked.*

### ACT 2 — "Hafengold" · Freihafen & Rostwerk
11. **Moin, Kapitän** — Herbie sends Kai to Tamsen. Empty a container before customs opens it.
12. **Wolfsrudel** — Bulle Brückner's test: a street race through Rostwerk against his road captains
    (races unlock).
13. **Autoschieber** — Steal three premium cars from the export list and load them into the Eisenwölfe
    container (car export unlocks).
14. **Laborratten** — For the Lotsen: burn down an Eisenwölfe meth lab in the old steel mill
    (the rival pairs now pull Kai in opposite directions).
15. **MS Lauterstern** — The big Hafengold shipment arrives. Unmarked trucks — Ruhland's people — hijack it.
    Kai chases the convoy through the container terminal.
16. **Die Akte** — Lena has documents: Achterberg's companies, the port, the cash. And a six-year-old photo
    of Ruhland, Achterberg … and Herbie.
17. **Dende** — Ruhland's men grab Dende to force Kai's hand. Kai storms the scrapyard. He gets there too
    late: Dende dies in his arms. Ruhland knew where to find him — only Herbie knew the hideout.
18. **Onkel** — Kai confronts Herbie, who flees in his old Mercator. A long chase ends at the edge of a
    harbour basin. Herbie confesses everything and gives Kai the key to a locker: a USB stick with a copy of
    Achterberg's black ledger, the **Schwarzbuch**. *Choice:* let Herbie go, or not.
19. **Brücken hoch** — Every cop in Neuhafen is after Kai (six blue lights). He escapes north across the
    Lauter the moment Achterberg's campaign reopens the bridges for his rally. *Nordstadt unlocked.*

### ACT 3 — "Abrechnung" (Reckoning) · Nordstadt
20. **Wahlkampf** — Achterberg's rally on the Altstadt Marktplatz. Steal his campaign bus (his face on the
    side) and send it into the Lauter on live TV.
21. **Kronzeugin** — Get Dr. Petra Lindqvist, Achterberg's accountant, to the federal prosecutors in the
    Turmviertel while Kronwache hunts her.
22. **Gipfeltreffen** — Kai convinces the gangs that Achterberg will sacrifice all of them. Depending on
    respect, two to four gangs join the final fight.
23. **Privatbank** — The heist: Neuhafener Privatbank, Turmviertel. Ilse cracks the vault, Kai drives. Inside:
    the original ledger and Kai's 2.4 million — still in the bank's sealed evidence of a "donation".
24. **Schwesterherz II** — Ruhland kidnaps Lena to trade for the ledger. The exchange in the Stadtpark goes
    wrong; Kai chases Ruhland onto the Autobahn — no speed limit, no way out.
25. **Hafenkrone** — With gang backup, Kai storms Achterberg's villa in Sonnenhang. Achterberg escapes to
    the airport.
26. **Endstation NHI** — The only finished part of the airport is the private-jet terminal. Stop the jet
    before it leaves the runway.

**Epilogue** — Newspaper headlines (Lena's story *"Die Hafengold-Akte"*), Achterberg in handcuffs, the
Hafenkrone towers frozen as a construction site. Kai and Lena at their mother's grave. Gül's Grill. Kai
gets into Gisela, Dende's car, and drives. Free roam continues; the underworld knows the Falke's name.

## Gang side jobs (repeatable, from coloured phone booths)
Each gang offers small jobs (2–4 min) that raise its respect and lower its rival's:
* **Lotsen**: smuggling runs by van, "lost" containers, harbour patrol boats (later).
* **Eisenwölfe**: car export list, bike races, sabotage of Lotsen trucks.
* **Kiez-Kompanie**: protection collections, bouncer back-up, chases.
* **NEON**: pill deliveries against the clock, flyer-posting with a cargo bike, club-night security runs.

## Running gags & satire
* Construction sites everywhere; NHI airport "opening soon" since 2011.
* Pfandflaschen as hidden packages (50 in the city).
* Radio adverts for KryptoKraft, Hafenkrone apartments ("ab 2,4 Mio."), and a lawyer who sues everyone.
* Police vehicles politely signalling before ramming you.
* Achterberg's posters everywhere, increasingly vandalised as the story progresses.
